---
title: Relative strength index (RSI)
url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_strength_index
---
The relative strength index (RSI) is a momentum indicator that compares the size of recent gains with the size of recent losses. It ranges from 0 to 100 and is most often calculated over 14 periods using Wilder's smoothing.

Readings above 70 are conventionally described as overbought and readings below 30 as oversold. These are descriptive labels for recent momentum, not signals that a reversal will happen; RSI can stay above 70 or below 30 for long stretches during strong trends.
