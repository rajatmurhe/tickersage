---
title: Slippage
url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slippage_(finance)
---
Slippage is the difference between the price a trader expects to get and the price at which the order actually fills. It happens when the market moves between placing and filling an order, or when an order is large enough to consume several price levels of the order book.

Slippage tends to be larger in volatile markets and in assets with thin order books. Tight spreads and deep books reduce it.
