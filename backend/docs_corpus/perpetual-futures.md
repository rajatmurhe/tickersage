---
title: Perpetual futures
url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perpetual_futures
---
A perpetual futures contract (often called a perp) is a derivative that lets traders take a leveraged long or short position on an asset's price without an expiry date. Unlike a traditional futures contract, it never settles on a fixed date.

Because there is no expiry to pull the contract price toward the spot price, perpetual futures use a funding mechanism: periodic payments between longs and shorts that push the contract price back toward spot.

Perpetual futures are traded with margin and leverage, so losses can exceed what a trader expects if the market moves quickly, and positions can be liquidated when margin runs out.
