title: Stablecoin

---
A stablecoin is a cryptocurrency designed to keep a stable value relative to a reference asset, most often the US dollar. Fiat-backed stablecoins such as USDT and USDC hold reserves of cash and short-term instruments. Other designs use crypto collateral or algorithms.

Stablecoins are widely used as the quote currency on exchanges, which is why most trading pairs are quoted against USDT. A stablecoin can lose its peg if confidence in its reserves or mechanism breaks down.
