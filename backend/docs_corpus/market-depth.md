---
title: Market depth
url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_depth
---
Market depth describes how much volume an order book can absorb without the price moving significantly. It is usually measured by summing the value of bids and asks within a percentage band around the mid price, for example within 1%.

Deep markets can absorb large orders with little price impact. Shallow markets can move sharply when a large order arrives. Comparing bid-side depth with ask-side depth gives a depth imbalance, which shows whether resting liquidity is heavier on the buy side or the sell side at that moment.
