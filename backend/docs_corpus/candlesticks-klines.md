---
title: Candlesticks and klines

---
A candlestick (called a kline in the Binance API) summarises trading over a fixed interval such as 1 hour or 1 day. Each candle has an open price, a high, a low, a close price and the volume traded during the interval.

Intervals are chosen to match the question: hourly candles describe the last day, 4-hour candles the last week, and daily candles the last month. Indicators such as RSI, moving averages, VWAP and volatility are calculated from sequences of candles.
