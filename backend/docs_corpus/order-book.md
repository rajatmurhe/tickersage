An order book is the live list of buy orders (bids) and sell orders (asks) waiting to be filled for a trading pair, organised by price level. Each level shows a price and the quantity available at that price.

The highest bid and the lowest ask form the top of the book. The gap between them is the bid-ask spread. The total quantity resting near the current price is called market depth.

Order books change constantly as orders are added, cancelled and filled. A snapshot only describes liquidity at one moment; large orders can be placed or pulled within seconds.
