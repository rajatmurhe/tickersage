title: Bid-ask spread

The bid-ask spread is the difference between the highest price a buyer is willing to pay (the best bid) and the lowest price a seller is willing to accept (the best ask) for an asset.

A narrow spread usually signals a liquid market with many participants, while a wide spread signals thinner liquidity and a higher cost of trading immediately. Spreads are often quoted in basis points (bps) relative to the mid price, where 1 basis point equals 0.01%.

Spread = best ask minus best bid. Spread in bps = spread divided by the mid price, times 10,000. The mid price is the average of the best bid and best ask.
