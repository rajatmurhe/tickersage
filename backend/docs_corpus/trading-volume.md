---
title: Trading volume

---
Trading volume is the amount of an asset traded over a period. On an exchange it can be expressed in the base asset (for example BTC) or in the quote asset (for example USDT). Quote volume is easier to compare across assets because it is in a common currency.

Rising volume during a price move is often taken to mean more participation in that move. Volume reported by one exchange covers only that exchange; aggregators such as CoinGecko report volume summed across many venues, so the two figures are not directly comparable.
