---
title: Market and limit orders
url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_(exchange)
---
A market order executes immediately at the best available prices in the order book. It guarantees execution but not the price, so it can suffer slippage in thin markets.

A limit order sets the worst price a trader will accept. It only executes at that price or better, so it may fill partially or not at all. Limit orders that rest in the book add liquidity; market orders remove it. A stop order becomes active once the price reaches a trigger level.
