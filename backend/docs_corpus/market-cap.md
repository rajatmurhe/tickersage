---
title: Market capitalization
url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_capitalization
---
Market capitalization is the total value of an asset's circulating supply, calculated as the current price multiplied by the number of coins or tokens in circulation. It is used to rank cryptocurrencies by size.

Fully diluted valuation (FDV) uses the maximum or total supply instead of circulating supply. Market cap is not the amount of money that has flowed into an asset, and thinly traded tokens can show large market caps that would be hard to realise.
