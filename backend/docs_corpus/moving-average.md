title: Moving average

---
A moving average smooths price data by averaging closing prices over a rolling number of periods. A simple moving average (SMA) gives every period equal weight; an exponential moving average (EMA) gives more weight to recent periods.

Common lengths are the 20-period and 50-period averages. Comparing the current price with a moving average shows whether price is above or below its recent average level. Moving averages lag price because they are built from past data.
