---
title: Maximum drawdown
url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drawdown_(economics)
---
A drawdown is a decline from a previous peak to a later trough, usually expressed as a percentage of the peak. The maximum drawdown is the largest such decline within a given window.

Maximum drawdown is a measure of downside risk over a period: it shows the worst peak-to-trough loss someone would have seen in that window, regardless of how the period ended.
