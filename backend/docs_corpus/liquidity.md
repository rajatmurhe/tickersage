---
title: Market liquidity
url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_liquidity
---
Market liquidity is how easily an asset can be bought or sold quickly without moving its price much. Signs of high liquidity include high trading volume, tight bid-ask spreads and deep order books.

Large cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin and Ethereum are generally far more liquid than small tokens. Liquidity also varies by venue, trading pair and time of day.
