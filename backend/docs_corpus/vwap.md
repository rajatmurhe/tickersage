---
title: Volume-weighted average price (VWAP)
url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volume-weighted_average_price
---
The volume-weighted average price (VWAP) is the average price of an asset over a period, weighted by how much volume traded at each price. It is calculated as the sum of price times volume divided by total volume. A common approximation uses each candle's typical price, the average of its high, low and close.

VWAP is used as a benchmark for the average price paid during a period. Comparing the current price with VWAP shows whether the asset is trading above or below the volume-weighted average for that window.
