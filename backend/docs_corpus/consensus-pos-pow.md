title: Proof of work and proof of stake

Proof of work and proof of stake are mechanisms blockchains use to agree on which transactions are valid. In proof of work, used by Bitcoin, miners compete to solve computational puzzles and the winner adds the next block. In proof of stake, used by Ethereum since 2022, validators lock up (stake) coins and are chosen to propose and attest to blocks.

Proof of stake uses far less energy than proof of work. In both systems, participants who act dishonestly risk losing resources: electricity spent in proof of work, or staked coins in proof of stake.
