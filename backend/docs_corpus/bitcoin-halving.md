---
title: Bitcoin halving
url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitcoin_halving
---
The Bitcoin halving is a scheduled event, roughly every four years or 210,000 blocks, in which the reward for mining a new block is cut in half. It reduces the rate at which new bitcoins are created.

Bitcoin's total supply is capped at 21 million coins, and halvings continue until the block reward reaches zero. Halvings change new supply issuance; their effect on price is debated and is not mechanical.
