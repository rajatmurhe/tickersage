---
title: Leverage and liquidation
url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leverage_(finance)
---
Leverage means using borrowed funds to take a position larger than one's own capital. With 10x leverage, a 1% price move changes the position's value by about 10% of the trader's margin.

If losses reduce the margin below the exchange's maintenance requirement, the position is liquidated: the exchange closes it automatically. Large clusters of liquidations can accelerate price moves, because forced selling or buying adds to the move that triggered them.
